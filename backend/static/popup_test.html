Let's create something beautiful together! Hello, I'm Clementina Phillips, an award-winning creative arts facilitator and textile artist hailing from Australia. I help people discover their creative voice through therapeutic art workshops in a warm, supportive environment. I specialise and have extensive experience in working with people facing various challenges— neurological rehabilitation, dementia, mental health concerns, social anxiety, and physical disabilities . Every session is delivered with patience, care, and understanding of each person's unique needs. Together, we explore creative techniques like painting, collage, printing, and mark-making, drawing inspiration from nature and the community around us. I facilitate both individual work and large group projects that build connection, confidence, and joy through shared creativity. Because creativity isn't just about making art—it's about discovering yourself and connecting with others. Let's create something beautiful together!
